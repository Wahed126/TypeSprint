Keyboard and scoring

Ctrl+R restarts an unfinished round. Tab and Shift+Tab move focus. Click Play again, or press Tab then Space or Enter. Continued typing after expiry leaves results visible. Code uses four spaces per indent and Enter for new lines.

WPM uses matching characters in the final text, divided by five and elapsed minutes. Accuracy counts all character-entry attempts, including corrected mistakes. Backspace and navigation do not count. Paste counts as text input; undo and redo are disabled.

Survival starts at 30 seconds. A correct word and its following space earn one second, once per word position. Practice finishes when you enter the passage’s character count; mistakes are allowed.

Leaving Play or switching browser tabs keeps the clock running. Restart discards unfinished rounds. Completed rounds are saved even if you restart at the deadline.
